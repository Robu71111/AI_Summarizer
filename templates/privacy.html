Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 17, 2026

Your privacy matters. We collect minimal data and never sell your information.

1. Information We Collect

AI Summarizer is designed with privacy in mind. Here's what we handle:

  • Text content you submit — text, uploaded files, and URLs are sent to our AI processing service to generate summaries. This content is processed in real-time and is not stored on our servers after the summary is generated.
  • Local storage data — your recent summary history, theme preferences, and settings are stored locally in your browser using localStorage. This data never leaves your device.
  • Basic analytics — we may collect anonymized usage data such as page views and feature usage to improve the service. No personally identifiable information is collected.

2. How We Use Your Information

The text you submit is used solely to generate your requested summary. It is sent to third-party AI model providers (via OpenRouter) for processing. We do not:

  • Store your submitted text after processing
  • Use your content to train AI models
  • Share your content with any third parties beyond the AI processing provider
  • Track or profile individual users

3. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • OpenRouter — routes AI requests to various model providers for text summarization and translation. Their privacy policy applies to content processed through their service.
  • Vercel — hosts the application. Standard web server logs may be collected.
  • Google Fonts and Font Awesome — for typography and icons, loaded via CDN.

4. Data Security

All communications between your browser and our service are encrypted using HTTPS/TLS. We do not maintain user accounts or store passwords. File uploads are processed in memory and discarded immediately after text extraction.

5. Your Rights

Since we don't store personal data on our servers, there is minimal data to manage. You can clear your local summary history at any time using the "Clear" button in the Recent Summaries section. Clearing your browser's localStorage will remove all locally stored preferences and history.

6. Cookies

AI Summarizer does not use cookies. All user preferences are stored using your browser's localStorage, which is entirely client-side and not transmitted to any server.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated revision date. Continued use of the service after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
